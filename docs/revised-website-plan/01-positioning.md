# 01 — Positioning

## 1. The position in one sentence

> I'm {{FOUNDER_FULL_NAME}}. I build websites, apps and AI systems for businesses, and I help them get found by people and by AI assistants.

The site speaks as one person. It is not an agency, a studio or a team. The homepage should read like a founder introducing their own work.

## 2. Founder-first rules

- **One voice:** first person ("I"). Never "we", "our team" or "our experts". Solo work is stated as solo work.
- **One face:** the same headshot, full name and location on the homepage, About, every service page and every verify link.
- **Specific over broad:** each service page names what I build, the tools I use and what the client gets at handover.
- **No agency signals:** no logo wall, no "trusted by" strip, no team page, no "book a discovery workshop" language.
- **Case studies say what I did.** "I built", "I wrote", "I connected". Not "the team delivered".

## 3. The three pillars

The homepage and the top-level menu are organised by what the client wants done, in three verbs.

| Pillar | Promise (one line) | Services under it | Proof available today |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Develop** | I build the product. | Web development · Mobile apps · SaaS products | None confirmed. See 07, O2 |
| **Automate** | I run the repetitive work in the background. | AI intake & speed-to-lead · Document intake & chase · Audit & sprint · Care plans | Internal build (LedgerLine), planned demo builds from research §6 |
| **Grow** | I make sure people, and AI assistants, find and cite you. | SEO · AEO & GEO | This site, once indexed. No results are claimed before they exist |

Pillar order is fixed on the homepage: Develop → Automate → Grow. Development is the entry point, automation is the strongest offer, growth is what keeps the rest visible.

## 4. Where the law-firm niche fits

Law firms are the depth of the site, not its front door.

| Place | How the niche appears |
|---|---|
| Homepage | One "Industries" row, one line, one link. Not in the hero, not in the H1 |
| Automate menu | Industries → Law firms |
| Law-firm page | `/services/automation/industries/law-firms`. Full niche page |
| Blog | A topic cluster of about 10 posts (see 05) |
| Develop and Grow | Law sub-pages added later, once the cluster has content and the law page is indexed: `/services/develop/web-development/industries/law-firms` and `/services/grow/seo/industries/law-firms` |
| Accounting | Reserved slot, not built. Built only if the Jan 2027 niche decision from research picks it (07, O5) |

Rule: niche words (intake, Clio, consults, client documents) appear on niche pages and niche posts only. The homepage and pillar pages stay general.

## 6. Voice guardrails (updated)

Keep the research word ban (revolutionary, game-changing, 10X, unleash, supercharge, cutting-edge, seamless, AI employee, fully autonomous, guaranteed results).

Add these:

- Avoid "solutions", "end-to-end digital transformation", "one-stop shop", "partner with us".
- No "guaranteed rankings", "guaranteed leads" or "guaranteed citations" for SEO, AEO or GEO. Say what I do and what I measure.
- Say what I don't do. Example: "Not my thing: native mobile apps without a clear brief." Keep the honesty of the research.
