How does LedgerLine work?

Three steps, from the moment a document arrives to the status board.

  1. 01

    Document received

    A document arrives through the channel you choose.

  2. 02

    Classified and filed

    It is read, given a type, and filed in the right place.

  3. 03

    Status board updated

    The board shows what has arrived and what is still missing.

Every step is logged, and every failure alerts a person.

LedgerLine questions

Is LedgerLine a client project?

No. LedgerLine is our own internal build, now in its third version.

Can it work with our document types?

Tell us what you receive on the call, and we'll say whether it fits.

What happens if a step fails?

Every failure alerts a person, and every step is logged, so we can see what happened and fix it.

Can you build something like this for us?

Yes. We agree a written scope and a fixed price before work starts, and it runs in accounts you own.
